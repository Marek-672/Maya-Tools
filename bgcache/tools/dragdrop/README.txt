bgcache: install

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (keep install_bgcache.py next to the "module" folder).
2. Start Maya (2024 or newer) and drag install_bgcache.py into a viewport.
3. Click the new bgcache button on your shelf: bgcache starts and the player opens.
   Right-click the button for Stop, Restart and Self-test.

It installs for every Maya 2024+ on this computer, in your own Maya folder.
Drag it in again to update or uninstall. Nothing else on your computer changes.
