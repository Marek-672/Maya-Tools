BGCache · free beta · Maya 2024+

Edit a key.Watch it play.

Heavy rigs can't play in real time, so you playblast again and again. BGCache does it for you in the background: a hidden second Maya re-playblasts only the frames your edit changed, the one you're on first, and a player plays the shot at full speed with sound while you keep animating.

How it works

Three programs on your own computer, passing small files to each other. No network, no server, and your scene file is never saved.

Your Maya

You animate as usual

Your Maya watches your key edits and sends just the changed curves when you let go of the mouse.

Hidden Maya

It re-renders the changes

A second Maya with a copy of your scene applies the edit and playblasts only the frames it changed, starting with the one you're on.

Player

The player plays it

A separate window plays the cached frames at full speed with sound, and follows your Maya's time slider and hotkeys.

40506070

Made for how animators work

The player follows your own Maya hotkeys: step frame, step key, play, scrub.

Draw over playback

Thumbnail your next pass, or mark arcs and spacing, right over the moving shot.

  • Pen and eraser with tablet pressure, layers, hold and onion skin
  • Paper: fades the picture so drawings read
  • Export to Maya's Blue Pencil

Focus range

Working on one bounce? Drag the two handles on the cache bar, or press I and O, and BGCache caches only that part first. Playback still covers the whole shot, and Maya's time slider isn't touched.

Safe for your shot

  • Your scene file is never saved or changed. Unsaved changes go to a private copy.
  • The hidden Maya has its own preferences folder, so yours are never touched.
  • Clear warnings when something needs you, for example the graphics card running out of memory.
  • Refresh, Reload scene, Re-render all and Pause, all in the player.

Install in a minute

It installs as a Maya module in your own Maya folder and never edits userSetup.py or Maya.env.

  1. Download the zip for your system from Releases and unzip it.
  2. Drag install_bgcache.py into a Maya viewport and choose Install. No restart needed.
  3. Click the new bgcache shelf button. Pick a camera and it starts caching.

To update, drag the new version's installer in the same way (your old copy is kept). The same installer also uninstalls.
