How it works

Three programs on your own computer, passing small files to each other. No network, no server, and your scene file is never saved.

Your Maya

You animate as usual

Your Maya watches your key edits and sends just the changed curves when you let go of the mouse.

Hidden Maya

It re-renders the changes

A second Maya with a copy of your scene applies the edit and playblasts only the frames it changed, starting with the one you're on.

Player

The player plays it

A separate window plays the cached frames at full speed with sound, and follows your Maya's time slider and hotkeys.

Made for how animators work

Three things, and you use them all the way through the shot.

01 · Live playback

Playblast without playblasting

Every edit plays back in real time, so you're always looking at the real timing. That's where spline and polish get their power: every small tweak, seen at speed straight away.

  • Focus range: drag the handles or press I and O to cache just the section you're on, and Play loops it
  • The frame you're on re-caches first
  • Your time slider's sound, in sync
02 · Drawing

Draw over it, and swap drawings with Maya

One drawing tool for the whole shot, right over the moving picture.

  • Blocking: ideas and stronger poses
  • Polish: arcs, spacing, overlap and smears
  • Notes: make them, bring in other people's drawings, and keep a saved history
  • Round trip with Maya: export to Blue Pencil, or bring Blue Pencil drawings in over the playback
03 · Continuity Full edition

See it in the cut

Your shot plays between the shots around it, so you can check the hookup and review it in context. Draw your notes right on it.

  • Set up continuity: show it one movie of your shot and it finds every other shot's newest version
  • Footage all over the place? Point it at folders and it searches them
  • Drop in an edit (EDL or XML) and shots follow the cut
  • For one person or a team: shared drawings and one setup

Then install it in a minute

  1. Unzip the file you downloaded.
  2. Drag DRAG_ME_INTO_MAYA.py into a Maya viewport and choose Install. No restart needed.
  3. Click the new bgcache shelf button. Pick a camera and it starts caching. Full edition: click Continuity and show it one movie of your shot.

It installs as a Maya module in your own Maya folder and never edits userSetup.py or Maya.env. To update, drag the new version's file in the same way (your old copy is kept); the same file also uninstalls.
